# PhonePointer — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 9 November 2026_

PhonePointer does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

- **No accounts.** The app has no sign-up, login, or profile.
- **No analytics or ads.** No tracking SDKs, no advertising identifiers, no third-party analytics.
- **Everything stays on your devices.** Settings (paired Macs, sensitivity, scroll speed) are stored
  locally on the iPhone. Pointer, click, scroll and keyboard commands travel directly to the paired Mac
  over Bluetooth; they never pass through a server.
- **Bluetooth.** The permission is used only to find and connect to the Mac running the companion app.
- **Mac companion download.** The companion app is downloaded from a GitHub Releases page. GitHub may
  log the download as any web server does; that is outside this app.

Questions or requests: open an issue at <https://github.com/michelevettone/phonepointer-download/issues>.
