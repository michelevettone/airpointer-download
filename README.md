# Air Pointer downloads

The free companion app for **Air Pointer** on iPhone: point the phone and the cursor on your Mac or PC follows.

- **macOS** — [AirPointerReceiver.zip](https://github.com/michelevettone/airpointer-download/releases/latest/download/AirPointerReceiver.zip) (macOS 14 or later, notarized)
- **Windows** — [AirPointerWindows.zip](https://github.com/michelevettone/airpointer-download/releases/latest/download/AirPointerWindows.zip) (Windows 10/11 with a Bluetooth LE adapter)

Everything travels directly over Bluetooth. No account, no cloud.
